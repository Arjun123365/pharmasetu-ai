## Step 2 — KPI validation and synthetic realism

Root causes confirmed by inspecting the existing model and the initial seed:

- `(productIndex + locationIndex) % 6` cycled every SKU through the same six coverage scenarios. Therefore all 30 products had at least one shortage and one stock-out; the product-level figures looked like a contradictory partition even though they were overlapping affected-SKU counts.
- The previous “low stock” calculation included every positive non-Healthy position, combining Critical and Warning.
- The previous “expiry-risk batches” KPI combined 30 stocked expired batches with 150 in-date batches due within 90 days. It did not distinguish these materially different groups.
- Approved requests were always deducted in full as incoming, even if their stock was subsequently received. The revised optional receipt matching reconciles that outstanding balance.

| Measure | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active SKUs | Distinct products, 30 | Same definition; separate from positions |
| Stock scope | Implicit 30 × 6 | Explicit 180 SKU-location positions, filtered denominator shown |
| Shortage categories | Critical included zero stock; “low” card combined Critical and Warning, counting affected products | Four mutually exclusive position categories: Out of Stock, Critical, Low Stock, Healthy |
| Expiry | Expired plus future due in 90 days, one combined KPI | Separate stocked expired batches and stocked in-date batches due within 90 days; exclusive summary bands plus clearly cumulative filters |
| Availability | Rounded stocked-position share, but near overlapping SKU KPIs | Same mathematical share, with explicit numerator/denominator, N/A empty scope and no fulfilment claim |
| Incoming | Full approved quantity regardless of receipt | Outstanding approved quantity minus matched receipts; unrelated adjustments do not change incoming |

The revised seed independently hashes demand, coverage and expiry streams instead of applying the same six scenarios to each product. It includes genuine zero demand, slow-moving items, shortages, healthy buffers, near expiry and expired stock. **It does not hard-code KPI totals.** At generation and validation on 9 November 2026, the unmodified revision-2 seed yielded:

- 30 SKUs, 180 positions, 360 batches, 2,160 weekly demand observations.
- 110 Healthy + 33 Low Stock + 24 Critical + 13 Out of Stock = 180 positions.
- 68,822 usable packs; availability = 167 / 180 = 92.78%, displayed as 93%.
- 10 stocked expired batches (1,708 packs), separate from 62 stocked in-date batches due within 90 days (14,340 packs).
- Future exclusive bands: 30 batches due 0–30 days, 16 due 31–60 days, 16 due 61–90 days. These sum to 62; cumulative 30/60/90 filters must not be added.

These are a synthetic snapshot, not targets or real distributor statistics. Expiry exposure naturally changes as calendar dates advance. Existing visitors retain their original seed and transactions until they explicitly confirm Reset demo data; they should not expect the new example totals merely by reloading.
